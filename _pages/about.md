<span class='anchor' id='about-me'></span>

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, South China University of Technology (SCUT), advised by [Prof. Xuemiao Xu](https://www2.scut.edu.cn/cs/2017/0629/c22284a328094/page.htm) and co-supervised by [Prof. Shengfeng He](https://shengfenghe.github.io/). Before that, I received my bachelor's degree from SCUT.

My research interests lie in **3D generative modeling, 3D computer vision, and computer graphics**. In particular, I focus on **3D mesh generation**, with the goal of modeling structured, high-quality, and controllable 3D geometry. I have published papers at major conferences and journals, including **CVPR, ICLR, ACM Multimedia, IEEE TPAMI, and IEEE TVCG**.

I expect to graduate in <strong>2027</strong>. I am currently 
<span style="color:#d9534f; font-weight:600; text-decoration:underline;">looking for <strong>research internship, full-time, and postdoctoral opportunities</strong></span> in 3D generative modeling and computer graphics.
